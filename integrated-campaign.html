Selected work / Brand, print and campaign design

ADI Free Shipping

A recognizable campaign across print and digital

A promotional campaign designed to support sales and membership growth for the ADI digital store, with a single visual idea adapted to multiple customer touchpoints.

Project at a glance

From concept to applications

My contribution

Conceptualized campaign collateral including email, web banners, marketing tiles, digital signage and mobile app banners.

Design decisions

Shipping-box imagery and dynamic lines communicate speed; warm and cool contrast and yellow lettering give the offer a recognizable visual signature.

Deliverables

Campaign identity, web and email creative, poster and digital signage applications.

01 / Design in practice

A visual shorthand for the offer

The shipping concept gives the promotion a clear focal point. The campaign mark and contrast establish a consistent visual language before the work is adapted into individual placements.

02 / Design in practice

The same message in different formats

The supplied applications show how the concept changes scale while retaining its identity. Hierarchy keeps the limited-time offer and next step visible across web, poster and signage formats.
